# Centrifuge (kind `centrifuge`, one instance)

Meaning: the lock-free single-producer rings (1,024 slots in the engine) that hold events until the audio thread
drains them at the next render quantum. Drawn with 10 pockets. The spout is the write index, the release gate the
read index; in sync both run at once (packets drop into free pockets as they pass while the gate drains one per slat).

## Use in the assembly
- `import { build, LIVE, CSS } from "../parts/centrifuge/centrifuge.mjs"`
- `build(ctx, origin, { id: "centrifuge" })` — origin is the machine's axis on the case floor; everything is built
  relative to it. Tallest point: funnel mouth at origin z + 186 (pulleys and motor cable + 183), under the ≈ 280 case top.
- `page(ctx, { css: CSS + …, live: [LIVE, …] })` — **CSS is required** (funnel interior gradient, strobe, lamp glow).
- Boot: `FACTORY.define("centrifuge", MACHINES.centrifuge)`, then
  `FACTORY.machines.centrifuge.syncTo(() => FACTORY.machines.audio.quanta())`. `fn()` may return the conveyor's
  `quanta()` object `{ phase, period }` (phase wraps once per cleat gap = one 128-frame quantum) or a bare phase
  number; `syncTo(fn, period)` takes the period separately. With a period the rotor runs at exactly one pocket per
  quantum and the release is led by `status().lead` (≈ 1.09 s: leave the pocket 0.28 + spout 0.50 + fall 0.31) so
  releases come one per quantum at the belt's rate; the conveyor's `accept` then lands each on the next free cleat.
  Without `syncTo` it uses its own 0.42 s clock.

# conveyor (kind)

One module, `conveyor.mjs`: `build(ctx, origin, opts)` + `LIVE` (registers `MACHINES.conveyor`, one kind for both
variants; the instance picks its variant from its data). Standalone page: `build.mjs` → `conveyor.html` (+ `-light`):
a single-sided belt (like belt B) fed from a hopper drops parcels onto the audio belt; garbage leaves the audio belt
at its head drum onto a second single-sided belt (like belt C) that tips it into a bin.

## Everything is relative to `origin`

`origin = [x, y, z]`: the belt centreline at its first drum (s = 0), on the floor the legs stand on (`z`). `s` runs
along `axis` for `length` (drum centre to drum centre); `+c` is the near side (`+y` for axis x, `+x` for axis y).
`top` is the belt surface height above `origin[2]`. No option takes a world coordinate:

| opt | top | loop | default |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id`, `axis` ("x"/"y"), `dir` (±1, travel of the top run), `length` | yes | yes | |
| `top` (z of the belt surface above the origin) | ≥ 20 | ≥ 2·drum + 22.2 (hanging parcels clear the floor by 8; clamped with a console warning) | 60 / 92 |
| `width` | ≥ parcel lane + 4 (12 on x, 14 on y; clamped) | same | 16 / 18 |
| `drum` (outer radius) | – | ≥ 10; gearbox, torque arm, motor, hubs, pillow blocks follow it | 15 |
| `inAt` (distance along the belt from the origin) | yes | yes | 8 / 12 in from the tail |
| `outAt` (distance) | yes | – | the head drum |
| `readAt` (distance; read point and lamp) | – | yes | L/2 |
| `feedFrom` (offset `[dx, dy, dz]` from the origin, start of an `accept` without `from`) | yes |

# packer

Boxes loose fluff (3 beads per packet) into post-style packets and drops them on belt B.

## What it is

A small packaging cell on a raised deck (four legs to the case floor, stringers, lower shelf with an air
receiver and a vacuum pump). Beads roll off belt A into a three-pocket gutter (photo-eyes light up React blue
as pockets fill). A **picker arm** (front-left, seen side-on) lowers a three-cup vacuum head (the lit accent),
lifts all three beads and drops them into the open carton at S1. A **walking-beam lane** (fingers rise through
a slot, push one pitch of 11, sink) indexes cartons through S0 erect → S1 fill → S2 fold + tape. At S0 a
pusher slides the bottom blank out of the magazine (the stack shows the coming colours, round-robin
control → graph → data) and the erector pad pulls it up into a carton whose flaps spring open. At S2 the
**folder/taper arm** (back-right) closes the minor then major flaps, runs a tape head across the top (strap
line grows, band fades in), then the next index pushes the taped packet off the lane nose onto belt B.

Parts: ≈ 112 recorded solids + 4 tubes static (legs, feet, heads, stringers, shelf, tank + saddles + gauge,
pump + motor, deck with T-slots and bolt rows, 2 turned lane columns with bolt circles, bed, rail + posts,
magazine posts/gate/hopper/cap/knob, pusher block/body/gland/ports, erector bridge posts/beam/cylinder/gland,
gutter post/floor/wall + eyes/stop/lip/near lip, 2 arm pedestals/turrets/slewing rings/cable bosses, picker
drive box, control cabinet with door, hinges, handle, label, valve manifold w

# M7 read path (scanner + belt D), with the M6 review fixes: result

*2026-10-14. Follows [M5-M11-plan.md](M5-M11-plan.md) M7 (census §1.5, layout §2.1) and the M6 review.
Verified by eye on web (the built-in browser pane, 800 × 600, light and dark, v2's `scanner(-light).html`
served read-only next to it, `assembly/shots/z-scanner-read*` / `z-scanner-D*`) and on the iOS simulator
(iPhone 18 Pro, landscape). Screenshots, the contact sheet and the recording live in the session
scratchpad, not in the repo.*

## Verdict

**Go.** The scanner reads as v2's: a hard-disk read arm on its pedestal behind the audio loop (floor
plate on feet, ribbed column, deck, kidney VCM plates on standoffs with magnets and crash stops, slotted
E-block arm with its load beam and slider, hub and nut, flex ribbon to the bracket, park ramp, white status
lamp on the graphite plate), the graphite preamp on four legs with fins, lid, input boss, 4-cell window
and lip, and the round cable from the flex boss down the floor to the preamp. The head follows a cleat
over the loop's near margin through its ±9 window and flies back in the 0.16 s seek at each read; the
lamp flashes mint, a mint pulse runs down the ribbon and the cable, each landed pulse fills a cell, and
the fourth pushes a mint packet out of the preamp's −y face across the lip onto belt D, which carries it
to a crate where the elevator will stand. 60 fps on the iOS sim at `fit` and in the arm view.

Go/no-go:
- **Head over a cleat, never over parcels:** tested (`scanner.test.ts`): while tracking, the head's x
  is a top-run cleat's x to 1e-6; at the window's start it waits ≤ 0.27 for the next; the slider's
  inner edge stays outside the parcels' lane on every step. By eye: `web-head-over-cleat-60.7-x12`.
- **Cells fill:** by eye (web and iOS), one per landed pulse, eased in 0.05 s / out 0.1 s (v2).
- **Packets leave the preamp lip onto belt D:** tested (hand-off step 0.000, 25 in 60 s) and by eye from
  a turned camera (`web-scanner-lip-D-look-light`). From the standard camera the push is behind the
  preamp and under the loop, as in v2's `z-scanner-D` (belt D is mostly hidden by the loop and the
  centrifuge until M8's elevator view).
- **§A holds for both controllers** (below).

Checkpoints: A `564e993` (sim + tests), B/C `534efa3` (machine, scenes, views), wrap-up in the last
commit. The M6 review fixes are `d1dc3d3` plus the card's fixed bottom inset in the wrap-up.

## What was built (M7)

**Engine:**
- `sim/scanner.ts` (v2 LIVE L148–334). **One source of reads: the loop's read point.** The loop counts a
  quantum and greys the parcel on it when a cleat passes its read point (as in M5); the scanner reads
  exactly then (`quanta().count`). The loop's `readAt` is snapped by `scannerReadAt` so the bottom-run
  cleat at the read point is in phase with the top-run cleat leaving the head's window: the head, placed
  from the real cleats (`phaseAt` at the window end, a pure function of the phase, so it is exact at
  t + acc), finishes a cleat at the instant of the read and seeks back from there. `createScanner`
  throws if the loop's read point is out of phase. On the line the read point moves from s 120 to
  113.7 (the greying starts 2.7 past the window end in x). Pulses run at 140 u/s along the ribbon (its
  rest length) and the cable (its exact filleted length, `SCANNER_CABLE`, the Tube's own centreline);
  4 landed pulses make one audio packet (`ledger.make`), pushed out over 1.1 s and handed on moving.
  Times are the loop's clock (travel / speed), so the order of stepping doesn't matter. Deterministic.
- Graphs: the line gains the scanner (step order: centrifuge, scanner, audio, …) and belt D (dir −1,
  `RATES.beltDSpeed` 28, top 24) tipping off its head drum into a sink at v2's elevator position;
  `beltDUnder(scanner, headY)` derives belt D from the hand-on point; `createScannerPage()` is v2's
  scanner page (a packet every `RATES.scannerFeed` 2.2 s onto the loop from a hopper, the scanner,
  a 60-long belt D).
- **Tube pieces** (`TubeSpec.range`): a piece of a tube between two arc lengths, with the whole tube's
  frames, so pieces meet ring to ring; a cut's rim is a join (`Mesh.joins`: the topology lets the edge
  border its own triangle, so no outline there) and only the true ends are capped. The cable is 30
  live pieces (7 long) that blend mint as the pulse passes (E2). `tubeCentreline` exports the fillet.

**App:** `machines/scanner/` — `Base.tsx` (plate on feet (`stand`), label, pedestal flange, ribs, column
with seams, cap, deck), `Head.tsx` (pivot and VCM, the moving `arm` group turned by −θ, ribbon strips,
bracket and boss, ramp, lamp with mint glows), `Preamp.tsx` (preamp on legs (`stand`), 4 live cells,
lid, fins, boss, lip; the cable pieces and shells), `Scanner.tsx` (one frame hook), `dims.ts`, `views.ts`.
`useNodes` (a fixed list of handles in one hook). Scenes: `line` extended (views `scanner-read`,
`scanner-D`, `arm`, `preamp`, `D-end`), a new `scanner` scene (`fit`, `arm`, `preamp`, `scanner`, `D`).
The line's readout counts audio packets (`held 6/10 · in sync · q 222 · 52 audio · 23 disposed`).

## §A still holds

The §A tests run unchanged for both controllers (no pocket entry outside index; held only falls to 0
before the next entry; held packets at `A + (k+½)·α`; releases forward only; `A` never decreases), plus

## Deviations

- **Reads come from the loop's read point**, and the loop's `readAt` is snapped to the head (v2 read the
  top run independently, and the conveyor greyed at its own `readAt`).
- **The push hands on moving:** v2 pushed the packet to rest on the lip's end (smooth, 1.1 s) and belt
  D's fall then jumped it 16 units in 0.18 s. Here the packet starts inside the box (4 deeper, so it
  never shows above the lid), accelerates (y ∝ u^2.4) across the lip and is handed on at the lip's end
  at 24 u/s; belt D's tail and in point sit where that motion lands (tail 3.3 past the lip's end, 4
  nearer than v2's, still clear of the lip; belt D 254.6 long instead of 249.9).
- **The ribbon** is 18 round-ended strips laid along v2's cubic each frame (a scalable pool), mint where
  a pulse runs; joints show as faint lines at high zoom. Its pulse timing uses the rest length.
- **The lamp lens** blends lit → mint with the glow (E2) and two mint glow discs, as the crusher's.
- **The cable pulse** blends whole pieces (7 long) by v2's gaussian instead of v2's per-chunk opacity.
- **Belt D ends in a crate** (sink) where the elevator stands in v2 A; `stand` on the scanner toggles
  the plate's feet and the preamp's legs; belt D keeps its legs (default on).
- **The card** keeps a fixed bottom inset for two text lines (see the review fixes).

## Problems and workarounds

- On iOS, rotating to landscape after launch left the canvas framed for the portrait stage (views
  appeared not to apply; drawing clipped); two JS reloads didn't clear it, restarting the app in
  landscape did. The first card fix (stage inset following the wrapped row) would also resize the
  stage after the canvas is up, so it was replaced by a fixed inset.
- The ribbon's first version (square strips, overlapping) drew a crease at every joint (a comb);
  round-ended strips meeting at the joints fixed most of it.
- The screen-recording skill isn't installed: recorded with the argent tools directly, moved out of
  `.argent/recordings` and transposed with ffmpeg (2622 × 1206, 40 s).

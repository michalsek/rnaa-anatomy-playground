# M1 line spike: result

*2026-10-13. Follows [M1-plan.md](M1-plan.md). Verified by eye on web (Chrome, DPR 1) and the iOS
simulator (iPhone 18 Pro, DPR 3). Screenshots are in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.*

## Verdict

**In-face lines hold the look. No §9 trigger holds after the tuning round, so no fallback.**
Next to the v2 crops (`z-instr-*`, `z-rotor-*`) it reads as the same style. The tach in
particular is close to v2's: graphite bezel, `mid` minors, `hi` majors, gap at the bottom. The
rotor, with its fading ribs, banded dome and back/front rim split, reads as a kit lathe.
The final style call is yours.

| trigger (at 1× fit) | verdict |
|---|---|
| T1: silhouettes lighter or thinner than creases or the v2 outlines | **no**. Outlines are `mid` at full width; creases are `faint` (half per face). The limb contour was half-width before tuning (see below). |
| T2: notches ≥ 1 px in 2+ presets | **no** after tuning. Before it, dashed rim outlines where a rim meets a limb, and a broken dome silhouette. |
| T3: silhouette width pulses during spin | **no** by eye. Measured on 4× rotor limb: 2.1 to 2.7 px around 2.4 px across frames. At 1× that is ±0.1 px at DPR 1. |

- `pnpm test` (18 tests) and `pnpm types` are green.
- No WebGPU validation errors on web or iOS.
- **Lines** read thin and even, not as smears.
  - At DPR 1, 0.6 CSS px draws as 1 px at 60 % alpha, like the SVG.
  - Corners are clean at 4× box corner.
- **Ribs** fade before the limbs, end on the band creases, and make the spin visible.

- **Bands** have soft, AA'd edges and no stairs at 4×, once the profile and tube bends are dense
  enough (see tuning).
- **0.5× thin**: lines thin out without breaking into dashes.

## Tuning round (one, within the in-face approach)

1. **Quad-partner edges.** A long thin quad's diagonal cut every edge line near its shared
   corner. That showed as dashed rim outlines next to limbs, worst at DPR 3.
   - Each triangle now also draws its quad partner's two outer edges.
   - Distances come from screen-space line equations and the fragment position.
   - `MeshBuilder.quad` records the pairs.
2. **Contour distance halved.** Near a limb the facing grows like √distance, so the first-order
   `f / |∇f|` is twice the true distance. Contour lines came out half-width (1.2 of 2.4 px).
3. **Lathe spline refinement.** Spans next to smooth knots are subdivided along a Catmull-Rom /
   Hermite curve, 8 pieces each. Before, the faceted silhouette (on a few coarse rings) and the
   smooth-shaded contour disagreed, so the dome outline tapered and broke.
4. **Tube fillets:** 16 samples per 90° instead of 8. This removes kinked bands and most of the
   contour streaks on the inner side of the bends.

## Deviations from the plan

- **Tach profile.**
  - The plan's tach profile reads as `(z, r)` pairs and is reversed. Taken as `(r, z)`, it is a
    bezel r 5.4, a face at 1.9, and ticks between r 3.2 and 4.6.
  - Scaled 0.8 so it fits the 10-tall box face.
- **Rotor.**
  - The flange grew to r 10.6 and the rib band is r 8.2 → 8.5. This way the r 0.9 tube can land
    on the flange without cutting the ribs.
- **Reversed profiles** are rejected in `lathe()`, by the sign of the swept volume. Topology
  can't see them: they are consistent, just inside-out.
- **No needle** (stretch goal).

## Known gaps (for M2+)

- Contacts are not lines yet: the tube start floats visually on the box face. The plan defers
  this to M2.
- The contour term can still leave faint short streaks at saddles, on the inner side of a tube
  bend, at 4×.
- Tube bands use the lathe band rule (plan §9 risk 7).
- On a phone in portrait, the "4×" views are 4× a width-limited fit, so they are less zoomed than
  on desktop.
- **Fast Refresh:** a refresh briefly re-ran the stage effect with the destroyed root. In Chrome
  that stalled the GPU process: the page froze, while the built-in pane and iOS survived.
  Retired roots are now skipped (`apps/app/src/gpuRoot.ts`).

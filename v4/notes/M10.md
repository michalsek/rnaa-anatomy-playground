# M10 the final factory scene (`factory`): result

*2026-10-14. Follows [M10-plan.md](M10-plan.md) (the user's §10 answer and the reveal redesign recorded there),
[M5-M11-plan.md](M5-M11-plan.md) §6 and the M9 code-quality review. Verified by eye on web (the built-in browser
pane at 1600 × 900) and on the iOS simulator (iPhone 18 Pro, landscape). Screenshots, contact sheets and the
recording live in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.*

## Verdict

**Go.** The `factory` scene is the whole thing: the phone (case, floating display with the React screen, the
dispenser under it) with every machine in its tray, the full graph from the dispenser to the speaker running live,
23 views with a card that follows them, an "i" sheet, eased view changes, a camera tour, and the user's
phone-opening reveal: the phone starts closed, the screen lifts, the machines rise out of their packed layer and
turn into the assembled factory; `conceal` plays it backwards. 60 fps on the iOS simulator at `fit`, in the views and
during the reveal.

Checkpoints: A1 `c9ed459` (graph, dispenser, ports), A2 `5691b9f` (window-sized stage, overlays, 16:9, bare),
A `12f1ff9` (phone blocks, static scene), B (120 s soak, below), C/D `e3155f1` + `1ed3325` (views, cards, sheet,
easing; stands), E `e723ee9` (the reveal), F `0cbfd01` (the tour and the recording), G (orbit: cut, below),
wrap-up last (the `line` scene off the chips, the §7.7 edge cases, one fix they found).

## The reveal (the user's redesign)

1. **Closed.** The display is the lid on the band (its OLED at z 36, unshifted). Twelve parts lie packed on the
   floor under it: the machines laid down (the elevator, the dispenser and the belts fit under 36; the speaker,
   packer, centrifuge and scanner lie 92, 59, 84 and 58 high and the clip cuts them at the lid's underside).
2. **The screen lifts** straight up 90 (0.8 s: what the clip cut stays under the lid), then floats to its pose
   (1.4 s).
3. **The machines expand** in `REVEAL_ORDER`: each rises off the layer, turns upright in flight at a height clear of
   everything planned before it, and comes down onto its spot (quintic ease, Euler angles to zero). The order keeps
   the later member of every docking pair landing over the earlier one (belts A and B before the packer, the loop
   before the centrifuge and the scanner, belt C before the loop and the crusher, the speaker before the elevator,
   the elevator before belt D); a scheduler delays a start until its whole motion clears every machine already
   planned and every one still packed. The dispenser goes last, up under the display. 14.4 s in all.
4. **Conceal** plays the same plan backwards.

The packed layout came from a constructive packer (big machines first, each kept off the finals of the machines
that land before it leaves, nearest to its own spot), then fixed by hand in `PACKED`.

Clip volume: machine fragments are discarded below the floor, above the lid's underside, and outside the case's
rounded plan below the band's top (36): above the band there is no wall, so machines may swing past the plan in
flight. The case and the display are never clipped. Cut faces: hidden under the closed lid; during the lid's
straight rise the cut stays at the lid's underside, covered by it from the camera; through the opening gap the
laid-down speaker's top reads as touching the lid for the first ≈ 0.3 s (noted, not hidden). Nothing rises out of the
floor. The sim is held at t = 0 through the reveal (and from the conceal on) and runs from the moment the dispenser
lands; a reveal from closed restarts it (nothing in flight, under the lid).

Tests (`reveal.test.ts`): the closed state inside the case on the floor, no packed overlaps; every pose T·R
(orthonormal, det +1), the packed pose at 0, exactly the assembled layout at the end, steps ≤ 3 units and 0.03 rad per
1/240 s; no AABB clash every 1/240 s (a docking pair only in the later member's descent); inside the case plan below
the band and under the lid once it is up. `store.test`: rare groups keep static slots, the identity write then
nothing.

## Deviations

- **The reveal** is the user's redesign, not plan §3's flat spread; the plan's `PACKED` squeeze, outermost-first
  order and plan-only lifts are replaced (see above). The packed layout is not "tight" in the 2D sense: every
  machine lies on the floor in one layer (36 high), so they cover most of the tray.
- **Meter cells** follow the scanner's delivered reads (as the preamp's cells), not `reads`.
- **Screen art:** the rings are flat half-annuli (not Tubes), drawn with no lines; the crossings are not v2's
  even-odd gaps. The glow is the glass's gradient band, not a Glow disc. Text, the ripple, the controls and the glare
  are cut (§1).
- **The dispenser's queue pitch** is a bead plus 0.3 (5.1; v2's 4.7 was for 4.4-wide beads); the tube holds 8 as
  M9's bead source (M11 decides); the dispenser falls at the belts' 420 u/s² (v2 900).
- **The stage** fills the window in every orientation and mode (the plan targeted ≈ 730 × 355 inside the card);
  the card is an overlay with header and footer bands, so the floating display ends under the texts.
- **Views:** 23 (fit, phone, case, 12 hand-offs, 8 machines); `centrifuge-audio` framed as v2's tight crop.
- **Orbit/pan/zoom (E11)** is not built (cut list item 1).
- **The `line` scene** is off the chips (the factory replaces it); `createLine` stays in the engine as the test
  bed (the §A, line and determinism tests), and `LineOf` draws any `createLine` layout for the centrifuge scene.

## Problems and workarounds

- The built-in browser pane only renders on a screenshot while hidden: long scripted waits there stall (the tour
  check ran on iOS).
- Once, a full JS reload on iOS (after editing plain modules) came up with "WebGPU unavailable" (react-native-wgpu:
  `install() returned true but RNWebGPU global is not available`); an app restart fixed it, and a later reload
  worked. Not reproduced.

## Out of scope (noted, not fixed)

- Glow discs are depth-tested (lamps behind thin parts lose their glow).
- `createShell` `MAX_DT` 0.1 vs the clock's 1/30.
- `flatten` walks every node.
- The root cause of iOS stale framing (worked around; the stage no longer resizes after start).
- `LINE_LAYOUT.audio.readAt` is unused.
- The argent watermark on recordings.
- Hermes `debugger-evaluate` doesn't await promises.
- Fast Refresh console noise on web.
- Cross-machine contact lines are lost under rare movers (none are drawn).
- The conveyor's `canAccept` ignores the lead for riding items (a held belt).
- react-native-wgpu's intermittent `RNWebGPU global is not available` after a JS reload (above).
- A Fast Refresh of a frame-hook module after the shell has stopped on an error does not restart the shell (an app
  reload does).

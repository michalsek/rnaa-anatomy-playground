# M11 review changes: result

*2026-10-14. Follows [M11-plan.md](M11-plan.md) (the user's review decisions). Verified by eye on web (a CDP Chrome
at 1600 × 900, DPR 1, and the built-in pane) and on the iOS simulator (iPhone 18 Pro, landscape). Screenshots,
contact sheets and the recording live in the session scratchpad (`m11-shots/`), not in the repo.*

## Verdict

**Go.** The factory opens full-bleed, the speaker is twice v2's size in the back-right corner, the input chain sits
further forward, the reveal is a short burst (4.5 s, was 14.4 s) with most machines in flight at once, the tour is
≈ 46 s (was ≈ 76 s), and the stage takes orbit, pan, zoom and a double tap on web (mouse, wheel, shift) and iOS
(touch). 60 fps on the iOS simulator at `fit`, through the reveal and after the 120 s soak.

Checkpoints: A `9122338` (bare default, meter, faster tour), B/C `729245d` (speaker, layout, stands, packed
layout, the reveal planner), D `cb56b8b` (the short reveal checked; the tour's hold after it), E `13a6de9` (orbit,
pan, zoom), wrap-up last (soak, perf, recording, these notes).

## What changed

1. **Bare by default.** `App.tsx` starts in `bare` (full-bleed); the card is opt-in through its chip. The screen's
   four meter cells are behind `SCREEN_METER` (`machines/phone/dims.ts`, false; the code stays).
2. **Faster tour** (`factory/tour.ts`): shorter holds (2–3 s), waits at 1.5–4× (4× is the clock's ceiling: 8 steps
   a frame at 60 fps), still event-gated; the opening step holds the assembled `fit` 1.5 s after the reveal (its
   length read from the plan). 76 → 56.7 s on iOS with M10's reveal (checkpoint A, measured; a headless replay of
   the steps on the graph agreed to 0.1 s), ≈ 46 s with M11's reveal.
3. **The speaker 2× v2's size.** `speakerGeometry({ scale })`: the driver (baffle, basket, surround, cone, cap,
   terminal) is drawn in a group scaled by k about the baffle's top on the axis; the stand grows half as fast
   (k 2 → posts at r 89, not 122, which would stand against the elevator); the throat stays packet-sized and
   reaches out from under the ring; the ledge, the in port, keeps its height, so the elevator's spout hand-off is
   unchanged in shape. The cone's kick scales with the driver (it moves inside the scaled group).
4. **Quarter-turn yaw for machines** (`sim/ports.ts`: `Located.yaw`, `quarterTurn`, `toWorld`): a hand-off turns
   the item's point and velocity from the sender's frame to the receiver's. The speaker and the crusher take a
   `yaw`; their blocks draw everything (frame hook, pools) in a turned `body` group, so the reveal's motion still
   sits on the root. Views turn with their machines (`placeBox`), MACHINE_BOX is on world axes.
5. **Layout from knobs** (`assembly/factory.ts`: `FactoryKnobs`, `factoryLayout`, `layoutFor`): `V2_KNOBS` give
   v2 A's layout exactly (= `LINE_LAYOUT`, tested), `M11_KNOBS` the factory's. `LINE_LAYOUT` stays the test bed.
6. **The reveal, short and mostly simultaneous** (`assembly/reveal.ts`; see below).
7. **Orbit, pan, zoom** (`camera/orbit.ts`, `shell/gestures.ts`, `Stage.tsx`; see below).

## The reveal (checkpoint D; `sheet-D-reveal-{light,dark}.jpg`)

- **Packed layout** from a greedy one-layer packer (biggest first, each in its lowest orientation, nearest its
  assembled spot, inside the case's rounded plan, 3 apart). The 2× speaker lies 144 high (the centrifuge 84): the
  clip cuts them under the closed lid, and the lid now rises straight up 150 (was 90) before it floats off, so the
  cut stays under it.
- **Planner:** the machines start 0.35 s after the lid, 0.1 s apart (in the docking order), each rises, flies
  turning upright (1.2–1.8 s by distance) at a height clear of what rests under its path, and comes down. A plan
  is checked every 1/60 s and a machine is delayed while it would pass through a machine at rest (still packed or
  landed), come down before its docking partner, or rise above the lid once it floats; flying machines may cross
  (the user: some chaos is fine). Up to 12 move at once; 4.48 s in all (was 14.39). Conceal plays it backwards.
- **Test changes:** the no-clash test allows overlaps between two machines in flight only (≈ 8 pair-seconds of
  crossing boxes, by eye no long pass-through); closed state, rigid T·R, small steps, exact end, inside the case
  under the lid are unchanged; a new test asserts the plan is short (< 7 s) and simultaneous (all but two moving
  at its busiest instant).

## Orbit, pan, zoom (checkpoint E; shots `E-web-*`, `E-ios-*`)

- Drag orbits about the target (0.4°/px; elevation 10–80°); two fingers or shift-drag pan with the finger; two
  fingers pinch about their centre; the wheel zooms at the cursor; zoom stays 0.5–12× the fit (the line reference
  zoom); a double tap eases back to the current view. Any of them stops the tour (a plain tap does nothing).
- Lines keep scaling with zoom (the renderer's reference zoom stays the view's) and the light stays camera-fixed
  (`lightOf`), as designed in M1.
- iOS: the WebGPU canvas's native view passed on only the first touch (a pinch arrived as a one-finger drag), so
  the PanResponder sits on a transparent RN view over the canvas. Web: the wheel and the shift key come from window
  listeners, only over that pad (not the chips).
- For M12's scripts: `renderer.setCamera(state, { ease })` eases to any yaw, elevation (90° works: `basisOf` needs
  no roll), target and zoom; DEV `__v4cam` (`get`, `set(state, ease)`, `ref`, `reset`); `revealCtl` (reveal,
  conceal, seek, skip, restart) stays callable from scripts.

## Deviations

- The dispenser moved only 90 toward the screen's bottom edge (the input chain can't go further forward: belt B must
  reach the centrifuge's funnel from −y); in `fit` it is still cut at the top (§10 option a).
- The speaker's stand grows at half the driver's rate (the brief said a uniform scale or scaled dims: the driver
  is uniform, the stand and throat are not).
- Stands unchanged.
- The disposal was not turned (tried, reverted).
- The reveal's packed speaker lies 144 high (cut under the closed lid; the lid's straight rise covers it).

## Problems

- The CDP Chrome went `hidden` once (the window occluded) and its rAF stopped: restarted with
  `--disable-backgrounding-occluded-windows` (one Chrome throughout).
- After a Metro reload the iOS app once came up with "WebGPU unavailable" (the known react-native-wgpu quirk); an app
  restart fixed it.

## Out of scope (noted, not fixed)

- `createShell`'s `MAX_DT` 0.1 vs the clock's 1/30; `flatten` walks every node; Glow discs are depth-tested (as M10).
- The WebGPU canvas's native view on iOS is not multi-touch (worked around with the pad).
- `LINE_LAYOUT.audio.readAt` is unused (as M10).

# M6 garbage path (belt C + crusher), with the M5 review fixes: result

*2026-10-14. Follows [M5-M11-plan.md](M5-M11-plan.md) M6 (census §1.4, layout §2.1) and the M5 review.
Verified by eye on web (the built-in browser pane, 800 × 600, light and dark, v2's `crusher(-light).html`
served read-only next to it) and on the iOS simulator (iPhone 18 Pro, landscape). Screenshots, the
contact sheet and the recording live in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.*

## Verdict

**Go.** The crusher reads as v2's `crusher(-light).html` in both themes (same parts, same proportions:
skids, housing, flared hopper with liners, toothed graphite rollers, flywheel and rubber V-belt to the
motor on its slide base, sliding bearing block on its spring, chute with the slide gate, drawer bin with
the hazard band, control box with dial and amber lamp). On the line, spent parcels drop off the loop's
head drum onto belt C, which carries them over the hopper's back lip; the rollers eat each packet (it
tips over the lip, tumbles into the nip and is squashed while it sinks), the drive speeds up and
settles, the block kicks back, the needle steps one twentieth per packet, the lamp pulses with each
packet in the hopper, flakes slide out in seven lanes into the drawer, and at 10 packets the gate
closes, the drawer rolls out over the grate, dumps, rolls back and the queued flakes go one at a time.
60 fps on the iOS sim at `fit` and in the crusher view. Nothing was cut.

Checkpoints: A `bbbcbea` (sim + tests), B/C `2ac6cc1` (static crusher next to v2; loop drop → belt C →
hopper next to `z-audio-C` / `z-C-crusher`), fixes and views `b1a8db6`, wrap-up in the last commit.

## M5 review fixes (phase 1)

1. **Slats on −1 belts** ran backwards: `uShift` is now `(travel + speed·acc) / total` (the band's u
   runs along the travel either way); a test checks a slat and a parcel move the same way on a −1 belt.
2. **Normals under axis scale**: `normalToWorld(model, n, inv)` = `M·(n / s²)` = `R·S⁻¹·n` with `s²`
   the squared column lengths, used for the fill normal, the per-vertex facing, `facingOf` (own,
   neighbours, quad partner) and the marks' normals. Checked on the gallery's squash cell (a rounded
   box, a sphere and a coil, turned and squashed): bevels and curves keep their bands.
3. **Loop reach window**: `uIn − cleatU` is wrapped into ±total/2. Wrapping made the old
   `(−0.5, 2.5)` gap window real for the first time (unwrapped it only ever matched near u = 0), and
   then `canTake` (which doesn't reserve) said yes for the same cleat on consecutive asks (11 late
   parcels). The intended window is the cleat nearest the in point: **±½ gap**, now `REACH` with the
   reasoning in its comment.
4. **Controller swap**: `SpinController.state` is `unknown`; `CentrifugeState` lost `spin`; the shared
   index/release/spin-down rules are functions every controller calls; a second model,
   `createRampController` (constant angular acceleration, rate-limited first-order lock), runs the
   contract tests (determinism, conservation, backpressure, §A ×3) under `describe.each` with the
   default; the phase timeline is the default's only. `topRig({ controller })`. `settle` moved to
   `sim/math.ts`.
5. **`sim/math.ts`**: `mod`, `wrapHalf`, `wrapPi`, `wrapSym`, `ease`, `smooth`, `clamp01`, `settle`,
   `springStep`, `clampOpt`, `relaxYaw` (all duplicates gone).
6. **One pose API**: every sim's `poses(cb: PoseFn)` gives `(i, item, x, y, z, yaw, pitch, roll,
   spend)`; `usePacketPoses(nodeId, sim)` returns a writer the machine's frame hook calls after
   `live.step` (it writes pose, style and blend every frame). `sink.items` → `poses`.
7. **Graphs**: the loop is created first, machines are added in step order, `syncToLoop(loop, from,
   v)` derives the fall from `audio.geometry`, and `factory.link(a, port, b)` throws unless `a` steps
   before `b`.
8. **Values from the sim**: hoppers read `source.from`, crates read the loop's drop / belt C's out
   port, `PacketPool` capacity reads `geometry.spacing` (`Conveyor.spacing(kind)` too), and `beltKit`
   takes the sim's `BeltFrame`.
9. **Sticky pool state**: `style(i, k)` resets the blend (mix 0); `PoolHandle` documents "write every
   per-instance field every frame, `blend` after `style`".
10. Dead code gone: `SPOUT_SLOPE`, `Z.packet`, the `status` scratch.
11. `FrameInfo.frame` counts the store's ticks; `live.step` compares it.
12. `sizeOf(kind)` / `PACKET_SIZE` replace `PARCEL` and the fluff size. **Strict receivers**: a
    receiver with `strict: true` (the centrifuge's intake) throws when it would have said no; out
    ports declare `asks`; `link` refuses a strict receiver behind a sender that doesn't ask (a belt
    without `hold`), and in DEV `send` checks `canAccept` first.
13. Tests: one line walk (conservation, landing phase, continuity), `advance` clamps on a bare
    factory, no `await import`, the log-cap test is gone.

## What was built (M6)

**Engine:**
- `sim/crusher.ts` (v2 LIVE L486+): packets by age (slide onto the lip, tip over it holding height,
  drop onto the back roller, tumble into the nip, squash while sinking; consumed when the crush ends),
  photo-eye drive settle (1.4 → 4.6 rad/s), underdamped block kick, lamp attack/decay, stepped needle,
  7 flakes per packet released one at a time into 7 lanes (queue spacing 1.9), the gate, the drawer
  (home → full → out 1.5 s → dump 1.2 s → back 1.5 s), chips where flakes settle. Deterministic (v2's
  `Math.random` flake seeds are a hash of the packet id). `CR` exports v2's dimensions for the blocks.
  `canAccept` (advice) is false until the last packet is off the lip and will have left the nip
  (`CRUSH_GAP` 1.0 s); belt C holds for it.
- `RATES.binDump` (10) and `RATES.crusherFeed` (2.4 s) join the rates.
- Graphs: the line gains belt C (110 long, top 40, `hold`) and the crusher at v2 A's positions;
  `createCrusherPage()` is v2's crusher page (a garbage packet every 2.4 s onto a short belt C).
- **E6** `stripes`: a line-set kind on box/extrude sides, `x = u·count − phase + v·slant` (the
  `LineSet` struct gains `slant`); Box and Extrude take `lines`.
- **E7** `helix({ from, to, r, turns })` for the Tube (the loop's take-up spring and the crusher's).
- **E9** step LOD: `circleSteps(r)` keeps the chord sag under 0.02 units (12..128 segments; was 8 per
  unit within 32..128); the Lathe and Extrude blocks cap explicit `steps` with it (`lodSteps`), the
  generators still honour an explicit count (the geometry tests).
- Conveyor queues never push an item back: items dropped closer than the queue spacing by a sender
  that doesn't ask stay put until the queue moves (they used to jump back up to 6 units).

**App:** `machines/crusher/` — `Housing.tsx` (base with `stand`, housing, rating plate, hopper with
liners and lips, photo-eye), `Drive.tsx` (rollers with teeth line sets, bearings, the sliding block and
the spring pool, flywheel with holes, three-piece pulley, motor with ribs, slide base, the V-belt and
its lacing clips), `Discharge.tsx` (chute, cheeks, gate, vibrator, floor rails, grate, the drawer with
casters, mat, walls, hazard band, handle, pile slab and chips), `Control.tsx` (cabinet with vents on a
pedestal (`stand`), dial with a moving needle, label, buttons, key, the lamp whose lens blends lit →
amber with amber glows, conduit, cables), `Crusher.tsx` (one frame hook; `stand` prop, default on),
`dims.ts`, `views.ts`. Scenes: `line` extended (views `audio-C`, `C-crusher`, `crusher`), a new
`crusher` scene (views `fit`, `hopper`, `spring`, `drive`, `ctl`, `bin`). The card stacks the hint above
the readout when they would meet (they overlapped in landscape).

## §A still holds

The §A tests run unchanged in substance for both controllers (no pocket entry outside index; held only
falls to 0 before the next entry, ≥ 3 cycles; held packets at `A + (k+½)·α`; releases forward only;
`A` never decreases), plus determinism, conservation and backpressure. The default controller's phase
timeline on the checkpoint A rig is identical to M5 (4.76 filling → 12.56 spinning up → 15.72 in sync
→ 25.38 spinning down → 26.97 filling); the line's is too (13.60 / 21.40 / 26.08).

## Deviations

- **The crusher takes one packet at a time** (`canAccept` + belt C `hold`): the loop drops a burst of 6
  one per quantum (0.57 s) while a crush takes 2.45 s, so v2's assembly would draw up to 4 packets
  overlapping in the hopper. Belt C now queues them (9.5 apart).
- **Flake chips spread about the landing point** (±3.5 × ±5): v2 put a chip exactly where each flake
  landed, which draws one row of chips per lane.
- **The pile** starts under the mat (only chips show the first ~15% of the fill) and spreads from the
  middle as it rises; v2 faded a full-floor slab in (v4 pools have no opacity).
- **Landed flakes** flatten and shrink instead of fading; the lamp lens blends lit → amber (E2) with
  two amber glow discs instead of v2's gradient overlay.
- **Lacing**: small clips (a pool) ride the V-belt's straight runs instead of v2's dots.
- The **ramp controller** is new (the swap test needed a second model); the default is unchanged.
- The **loop's reach window** is ±½ gap (see fix 3), not v2's 2.5 gaps upstream.
- `stand` on the crusher toggles the feet, skid rails, cross members and the control box's pedestal.

## Problems and workarounds

- Wrapping the loop window broke "canTake never refuses" (11 late parcels): narrowed to ±½ gap.
- Belt C with `hold` snapped parcels back up to 6.4 units when its queue formed (the queue limit
  assumed items were at least a spacing apart): fixed in the conveyor (never move back).
- On iOS a card layout change mid-session left the canvas framed for the old stage until a JS
  reload; a reload fixes it (not seen on web).
- The screen-recording skill isn't installed; recorded with the argent tools directly and transposed
  with ffmpeg (2622 × 1206).

*2026-10-14. Follows [M12-plan.md](M12-plan.md), §9 (the user's answers) and the user's changes made during

## Verdict

**Go.** One master, `loop-light-60.mp4`: 16:9, 1920 × 1080, light, 60 fps, silent, H.264. It is 3,655 frames,
60.92 s and 55.3 MB, and it loops seamlessly.

1. **Part 1 (web, frame-stepped):** the open factory, then the full M11 tour, the conceal, and the camera
   going top-down over the closed phone.
2. **Seam A:** the closed phone's screen becomes the iOS simulator's live screen.
3. **Part 2 (iOS, real time):** the camera pulls back until the screen holds a phone inside the phone, tilts
   to the factory view and opens the phone.
4. **Seam B:** the output zooms into that inner factory until it fills the frame, then dissolves into Part 1's
   opening frames, so the file loops.

Seam A SSIM is 0.9960 on the sources and 0.9947 in the master. Seam B is 0.9990.

## The outro and the final video

`final-light-60.mp4`: the loop, then a 7.5 s outro (4,105 frames, 68.42 s, 58.3 MB). The camera zooms out from
the loop's last frame onto the user's capture of a Claude Code thread, whose simulator panel shows the factory
app (`outro/claude-app.png`, 2560 × 1409). `loop-light-60.mp4` stays the seamless loop on its own.

- **The panel's screen**, found from its pixels (the paper's sub-pixel edges against the bezel along clean
  rows and columns): (1921.91, 124.91) 540.37 × 1176.18 px, aspect 0.45943 (402 : 874 = 0.45995).
- **Inside it:**
  - The screenshot's real status bar and the app's chip row stay as they are.
  - Below the chip row (110 pt) the screen is the app's paper, edge to edge. The bottom corners follow the
    screen's own profile, measured per row.
  - The footage plays in the film's 16:9 band: the full screen width, centred, (1921.91, 561.02)
    540.37 × 303.96. It is Part 1's frames from 30 on, so the tour keeps playing inside the panel.
- **The zoom** runs from the band filling the output (×3.5531: the footage 1:1, so the outro's first frame
  is the loop's next frame) out to the whole window at ×0.75.
  - It is exponential with smootherstep, about the point that stays still: (2436.1, 715.3) in the
    screenshot, held at (1827.1, 548.1) in the output.
  - The zoom takes 3.5 s, then a 4 s hold.
- **Fit:** 2560 × 1409 is wider than 16:9, so it is fitted to the width with an 11.6 px letterbox top and
  bottom in the window's own background (21, 21, 21). Cropping to the height would have cut the traffic
  lights and the window controls at the edges.
- **Checks:**
  - Outro frame 0 equals Part 1's frame 30 exactly. In the encoded file, loop end → outro start is SSIM

  - The thread text is legible in the final 1080p frame (`outro-final-text-100.png`).

## The user's changes during M12 (they override the plan)

1. **The phone's screen is an iPhone 18 Pro's (the stitch fix).** The first master used a cover framing at C0.
   That mapped the simulator's screen to a sub-rectangle of Part 1's glass, leaving static dark glass around
   the footage during the pull-out (thin bands on three sides, a thick one at the bottom). The user rejected
   it as a broken phone-in-a-phone.
   - **New glass:** the display's glass now has the simulator screen's shape: 402 × 874 pt with 61 pt corners
     (just inside the simulator's continuous corner, measured from a `--mask=alpha` screenshot).
   - **Case:** `PHONE_CASE` is 360 × 780.81, r 55.18 (it was 760 deep, r 44), so the glass is
     358.4 × 779.21, r 54.38, under the same 0.8 rim. The case grew 10.4 at each end: the machines, the
     packed reveal (tests) and the closed lid's clip still fit. `fit`, `case` and `phone` frame the longer
     case.
   - **The splash:** at C0, containing the glass now also covers the screen (`SIM_SPLASH` cover 1, dy 0;
     zoom 1.12165 pt/unit). The glass's corners sit on the screen's, and the simulator's screen is G
     exactly: (730.02, 40, 459.95 × 1000).
   - **The mask** is the glass's shape (r 69.79 px) inset 1 px, so Part 1's glass outline stays. During
     the pull-out the only frame around the footage is the rendered case's thin rim.
2. **The screen art is an iPhone's, everywhere** (`SCREEN_STATUS_BAR = true`; the user: the phone should
   look like an iPhone). The simulator's status bar can't be hidden: iOS 27 with the UIScene lifecycle
   ignores `StatusBar hidden` and `setStatusBarHidden`, and a window above the status bar's level (tried
   natively, reverted) doesn't hide it either. So the art recreates it, and the real one continues from it.
   - **Status bar:** the pinned bar (9:41 shown as "09:41", cellular 4, wifi 3, battery 100 % discharging)
     traced at C0 into 19 hole-free polygons (`statusBar.ts`, flat white `statusInk`). Rendered against
     real on the simulator: best shift 0.00 px, mean coverage difference 0.003.
   - **Dynamic Island:** flat black (`islandInk`), 94.8 × 36.8 pt, centre 32.3 pt down. It lands on the
     simulator's hardware island to the pixel (x 461–744, y 42–151).
   - **Home indicator:** 134 × 5 pt, 8 pt up.
   - **Gone:** v2's inner edge line on the glass.
   - **In the video:** after the pull-out the simulator's bar turns black over the paper and the Dynamic
     Island stays, as on a real phone.
3. **Lamps fade at the conceal** (film only, `filmCtl.lamp`). The centrifuge, scanner and crusher lamps' glow
   sprites are depth-biased toward the viewer and not clipped. A lamp lit when the sim is held showed through
   the closed lid, while Part 2 starts from a sim at t 0 with the lamps off. They now fade over the conceal's
   first second.
4. **The logo turns on the film clock.** It runs from 0 in Part 1 and from Part 1's last angle φ1 =
   15.99467 rad in Part 2. At F it is outside the band (checkpoint B), so the plan's freeze at angle 0 wasn't
   needed.

## What was built

**Engine**
- **`camera/film.ts`** (pure, tested):
  - `screenCamera`, `simCamera` and `SIM_SPLASH`;
  - `filmBand` and `filmFrame` (the 16:9 band minus a 2.5 % margin, as exact pads);
  - `filmLineScale` (the band's width over 1920);
  - `cameraLerp`, `smootherstep`, `projectCss`, `glassRect`;
  - `filmGeometry`: G, C1, C0, the simulator's screen in the output, the band, and S.
- **Renderer:**
  - `setClock(fn | null)`: eases read the film clock;
  - `setLines({ scale, ref } | null)`: `pxPerCss = dpr · scale · zoom / ref`, with ref pinned to the film
     frame's fit at 45/30;
  - `cameraFor(view)`.
- **Palette:** `statusInk` and `islandInk`, appended.

**Shell**
- The loop body is now `tickDraw`.
- `setManual(on)` and `step(dt)` (tick and draw, then `onSubmittedWorkDone`).
- Pass-throughs `cameraFor`, `setClock` and `setLines`.

**App (DEV only, off by default)**
- **`film/filmCtl.ts` and `FilmDriver.tsx`.** `FilmDriver` is the first frame hook in `FactoryScene`.
- **`__v4.film`:** `enable({ platform })`, `disable()`, `part1()`, `step()` (web; resolves when drawn),
  `arm({ logo })`, `go()`, `state()`, `screenRect()`.
- **Changes in existing modules:**
  - `revealCtl.skip({ hold })`, `reveal({ holdAfter })`;
  - `tourCtl.play({ from, reveal })`, `tourCtl.overrideView(fn)`: in film mode the tour's views go straight to
    the shell, eased on the film clock;
  - App: in film mode the snapshot forces light, bare, no chips and the film frame;
  - Phone: the logo angle and the tap pulse;
  - Display: the status bar and the island.

**Tools (`tools/film/`)**
- `cdp.mjs` and `eval.mjs`: a dependency-free CDP client.
- `capture-web.mjs`: Part 1.
- `still-web.mjs`: checkpoint B.
- `check-p1.py`: per-frame camera steps and determinism.
- `trace-status-bar.py`.
- `composite.py`: the tail (its resampler in `composite_lib.py`).
- `outro.py` and `final.sh`: the outro and the final video.
- `loop.sh`: the master and the 3× loop.

**Tests:** `film.test.ts`, 4 tests.
1. G at C1. At C0 the glass's corners land on the simulator screen's corners, the simulator's screen in the
   output is G, and G's corner radius is the glass's.
2. The band and frame are the same relative to the band on web and simulator, the band maps to the output's
   centre, and S fills it.
3. `cameraLerp`: exact ends, shortest yaw, monotone log zoom.
4. No frame jumps: per-1/60 s camera steps are bounded and one-signed along F → C1, the pull-out, the tilt,
   and the renderer's ease across ±180°.

`pnpm test` passes (124 tests) and `pnpm types` is green.

## Re-making the video

The capture Chrome is the user's Chrome with its own profile. It needs `--force-color-profile=srgb`: without
it, screenshots come in the display's P3 (#2fb8e6 came out as (80, 172, 223)).

```sh
SCR=<scratch>/m12
# 0. dev server on :8090 (pnpm web in v4/); the simulator app from Metro on :8090, portrait, not rotated
open -na "Google Chrome" --args --user-data-dir=$SCR/chrome-film --remote-debugging-port=9333 \
  --disable-background-timer-throttling --disable-renderer-backgrounding \
  --disable-backgrounding-occluded-windows --force-color-profile=srgb http://localhost:8090
# 1. Part 1 (≈ 5 min; don't save files in the workspace while it runs: Metro rebuilds)
node tools/film/capture-web.mjs $SCR            # p1.mkv, p1-state.jsonl, p1_last.png, p1-summary.json (φ1 = last.logo)
python3 -I tools/film/check-p1.py $SCR/p1-state.jsonl [another run's p1-state.jsonl]
# 2. Part 2: pin the status bar, arm, record, go (argent debugger-evaluate on Metro :8090)
xcrun simctl status_bar EBB4A340-5607-41A6-B36C-A9255815E16B override --time 9:41 --dataNetwork wifi \
  --wifiMode active --wifiBars 3 --cellularMode active --cellularBars 4 --batteryState discharging --batteryLevel 100
#   evaluate: __v4.film.enable({ platform: 'sim' }); __v4.film.arm({ logo: φ1 })   → save as p2-armed.json
xcrun simctl io EBB4A340-5607-41A6-B36C-A9255815E16B recordVideo --codec=h264 --mask=ignored --force $SCR/p2.mov &
#   after "Recording started" + 1 s: evaluate __v4.film.go(); ≈ 12 s later state().done → kill -INT the recorder
#   p2-summary.json = { armed: <arm's state>, state: <state() when done> }
xcrun simctl status_bar EBB4A340-5607-41A6-B36C-A9255815E16B clear
# 3. the tail (C2, zoom, dissolve; ≈ 1 min) and the master
python3 -I tools/film/composite.py $SCR       # tail.mkv, tail.json, c2_*.png
sh tools/film/loop.sh $SCR                    # loop-light-60.mp4, loop3x.mp4
```

If the status bar or the simulator's framing ever changes, re-trace it:

```sh
python3 -I tools/film/trace-status-bar.py <C0 screenshot> apps/app/src/machines/phone/statusBar.ts --ty <C0 target y> --zoom <C0 zoom>
```

Use a screenshot taken with `simctl io screenshot --mask=ignored` with the bar pinned.

## Deviations from the plan

- The phone's screen is an iPhone's and the status bar is recreated (above). The plan's R_sim, Δ, ×0.3919
  and S 4.0628 became G itself, ×0.38139 and S 4.1743.
- **Compositing** is a Python resampler (`composite.py`): separable Lanczos-3 matrices at the exact sub-pixel
  affine map, from the 4K Part 1 frame and the simulator frames. It replaces the 8K ffmpeg intermediate, so
  there was no 8K file to delete. It also drops the recorder's repeats instead of resampling with `fps=60`,
  which duplicated and dropped frames, and it matches the recording's colour.
- **The zoom ends where the dissolve starts:** 1.5 s zoom, then a 0.5 s dissolve at S. In the plan's version
  the dissolve began at E = 0.9, 16 % short of S, and ghosted.
- **Part 2 is driven with argent's `debugger-evaluate`.** A Node CDP client to Metro's inspector needs an
  `Origin` header (401 without one) and then closed with code 1006 right after opening, so that script was
  dropped. The recording waits about 6 s on the still splash before `go()`; the compositor trims it.
- **Seam A is a hard cut,** since SSIM is ≥ 0.98.
- **The capture hides Expo's dev indicator** (`body > :not(#root)`). It flashed a bundling badge into 3 frames
  when a workspace file was saved mid-capture.

## Problems

- **Status bar and Dynamic Island.** The simulator's status bar can't be hidden on iOS 27 with scenes, which
  led to the user's recreated bar. The simulator's Dynamic Island is drawn into the recorded framebuffer in
  every frame, and in screenshots only now and then; the rendered island is matched to it.
- **The first master was rejected** (the cover splash's dark margins, above): the phone was reshaped and
  everything re-made.
- **Argent's touch injection stopped** at the very end ("CoreDevice transport is dead"). Argent 0.29.0 is
  available; not updated.
- **Seam A, inside the screen,** is a touch softer than a normal frame step (0.979 vs 0.990). That comes from
  the simulator's H.264 at about 7.5 Mbit/s in the dark gradient.
- **The capture Chrome** first ran in the display's P3 profile; it was restarted with an sRGB profile.

## Out of scope (noted, not fixed)

- **In the normal app, a lit lamp's glow shows through the closed lid** (centrifuge, scanner, crusher). Glow
  sprites are depth-biased toward the viewer and not clipped by the reveal's clip volume. Their closed-state
  positions are 74–115 high, above the lid at 41.
- **Expo web's dev indicator** is a fixed div after `#root` that flashes on every Metro rebuild.
- **`createShell`'s `MAX_DT`** is 0.1 while the clock's is 1/30 (as before).
- **Social platforms re-encode uploads,** which may break gapless looping.

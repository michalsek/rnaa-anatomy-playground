# M5 shared sim, real conveyors, the centrifuge between real neighbours: result

*2026-10-14. Follows [M5-M11-plan.md](M5-M11-plan.md) §3 M5 (with §1.1–1.2, §1.9 E1/E2/E3/E4/E5/E8,
§2.2 and the user's answers in §6). Verified by eye on web (the built-in browser pane at
1600 × 900, plus one argent Chromium at DPR 1 for the v2 reference pages and a DPR 1 check) and on
the iOS simulator (iPhone 18 Pro, landscape), light and dark. Screenshots, the contact sheet and
the recording live in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.*

## Verdict

**Go.** The conveyors read as v2's `conveyor(-light).html` in both themes (same layout, same
parts, same motion: slats scroll, cleats round the drums, parcels hang from bails, grey at the
read lamp and drop at the head). The centrifuge runs its §A cycle between a real belt B and the
real audio loop, releases one packet per loop quantum onto a free cleat (the lock lands them
within 0.56 units of their cleat, no long bends), and hand-offs are continuous. 60 fps on the iOS
sim at `fit` and in a machine view; the line scene is 81k triangles. Nothing was cut.

## What was built

**Engine, `sim/` (plain TS, one fixed step for all machines):**
- `clock.ts` `createFactory()`: machines step in `add` order (upstream first: source, belt B,
  centrifuge, loop, sink), M4's `advance` (dt ≤ 1/30 s × speed, ≤ 8 steps, the rest dropped) and
  an uncapped `run` for seek and tests; `alive()` for conservation.
- `items.ts`: `ItemKind` (fluff, control, graph, data, audio, garbage) replaces `Kind = 0|1|2`;
  `packetStyle(kind)` maps a kind to the packet pool's style (explicit, `PACKET_KINDS` order); a
  ledger (`created = alive + consumed`). Items carry their yaw across hand-offs.
- `ports.ts`: `Receiver { canAccept(lead?), accept(item, from, v) }`, out ports and `link`; a
  hand-off converts local → world → local (machines are placed by translation).
- `path.ts` (E4): the belt frame (s, c, z; the y frame is a reflection, as v2) and the stadium loop
  (u → run, s, z, normal, tangent; no allocation), `tilt()` for the pool pitch/roll.
- `fall.ts`: v2's fall (keeps the sender's velocity, bends in a quadratic onto a moving target).
- `conveyor.ts` (top, v2 Cv L641–748): fall in, ease to speed with a hop, queue/`hold`, tip off
  the head drum; `canAccept` = the in point is clear.
- `loop.ts` (v2 Cv L752–939): cleats, parcels on free cleats, bails, read-point greying (`spend`),
  drop at the head drum as garbage, `quanta()` (read point), `phaseAt(u, ahead)`, `canTake(lead)`.
- `source.ts` / `sink.ts`: M4's burst feed (timer holds while the out port can't take an item);
  a sink that lets items fall into a bin before consuming them.
- `rates.ts`: **the tunable rates in one place** (source pattern, `HOLD` 6, loop speed 32, top
  belt speed 14), at v2's values (§6, Q5).
- `graphs.ts`: `createLine(LINE_LAYOUT | CENTRIFUGE_LAYOUT)` (positions from v2 A's derivations,
  §2.1) and `createConveyorPage()` (v2's conveyor page).
- `centrifuge/`: **split into `packets.ts`** (throat, drops, pockets, landings, release path,
  hand-on; owns the §A invariants: `put`/`accept` throw outside index) **and `controller.ts`**
  (`SpinController { state, mode, A, w, inSync, phase, syncW, step(h, io) }`; the default is M4's
  index/spin/down with the §A lock). Injected: `sync` (phase/period; the line locks onto the
  cleats at the in point, a fall after the hand-on), `canRelease` (default the out port's
  `canAccept(lead)` = the loop's `canTake`), `hold`. A new spin model is a new controller under
  the same tests.

**Engine, render and scene:**
- **E1** scrolling line sets: `Instance` gains `uShift` (the fill's u is shifted per instance);
  a new `band` geometry (a closed belt loop whose u is the sim's arc length) carries the slats.
- **E2** style blend: `Instance` gains `style2` and `mix`; every palette lookup in the fill (and
  in pool marks) blends toward the second style's material, so fills and lines cross-fade like
  v2's opacity overlay. Set through `PartHandle.mix` (parts declared `live` with a `blend` style)
  or `PoolHandle.blend(i, k2, mix)`.
- `Instance` is 96 B (was 80): no new binding, the scene layout stays at 8 storage buffers. The
  fill's flat varyings pack style/style2 and facing gradient/mix (13 locations, as before).
- **E3**: pools pitch/roll on a path (cleats, parcels, straps round the drums) and pools declared
  `scalable` take an axis-aligned scale (`POSE_FLOATS` 6 → 9; the bail straps).
- **E5**: materials `pkAudio`, `pkGarbage`, `fluff`, `mint`, `amber`, `rubber`; FX glows `mint`,
  `mintSoft`, `amber`, `amberSoft`.
- **E8**: `flatten()` reports the rewritten instance range; statics that move (under a moving
  group, or live parts) take the last static slots, just before the pools; the renderer uploads
  only that range (`queue.writeBuffer`, no host copy); a pool rewrites only up to
  max(count, last count). The line scene uploads 15.5 KB/frame on iOS (the table is 67 KB).
- DEV: `renderer.perf()` and `__v4perf()` (fps, rAF gaps, tick, flatten + upload, frame CPU,
  build stats).

**App:**
- `machines/kit.ts` (shared helpers lifted from the centrifuge's `dims.ts`), `machines/Packets.tsx`
  (one packet pool for every machine: 5 kinds, a strap top and bottom for hanging parcels).
- `machines/conveyor/{Top, Loop}.tsx` + `Parts.tsx` + `dims.ts`: v2's buildTop/buildLoop part by
  part (band with slats, drum cores, flanges with bolt circles, hubs, rails, idlers with nuts,
  pillow blocks, the graphite drive with ribbed motor, guides, lamp, label plate, legs/feet/braces;
  the loop's riveted truss, stubs, return rollers on hangers, take-up with a coil spring, torque
  arm). **`legs` prop on both, default on**; the centrifuge has **`stand`, default on**.
- `factory/live.ts`: one live object per scene (graph rebuilt on seek, ctl, readout, `__v4.sim`);
  every machine block's frame hook calls `live.step(frame)` first (the first call per frame
  advances the graph), so all blocks write poses from the same instant.
- `factory/Scenes.tsx`: `line` (now the default scene), `centrifuge` (short belt B + short loop,
  the M4 page) and `conveyor` (v2's conveyor page, checkpoint C); a hopper hides where the source
  makes packets, a crate hides where the sink takes them.
- `Stubs.tsx`, the old `centrifuge/live.ts` and the stub code in the sim are gone.
- Gallery row `belt` (E1–E3): scrolling slats on a band, cleats pitched round a band's drums, a
  packet blending control → garbage, a packet squashed by axis scale.
- M4 review leftovers: queued packets forget their pocket when the spin starts; `__v4.sim.seed`
  takes an integer 0–1e6 (else throws); the phase log keeps the last 200; `dims.ts` takes the
  spout start and direction from `CF.spout` (no recomputation).

Checkpoints: A `c23e7fa` (sim + §A tests on real belts), B `8ff2ac5` (loop, E1–E8, gallery,
blocks, scenes), C/D `189406f` (views; by eye), the hand-off fix `74b766a`, wrap-up in the last
commit.

## §A still holds

The M4 §A tests run unchanged in substance on the checkpoint A rig (real source, belt B with hold,
real receiver): no pocket entry outside index; from spin-up held only falls, to 0, before the next
entry, over ≥ 3 cycles; every held packet's pose angle is `A + (k+½)·α` during spin; released
packets move forward only (never back, never a whole turn); `A` never decreases. New: belt B hands
on only into an open intake (the tray's `accept` throws otherwise, and the test wraps it), the
front of belt B never passes the out point while held, and the receiver override holds every
packet for 6 s and the tray drains once restored. By eye (web, iOS, the recording): the whole tray
turns with its packets, the intake stays closed until spin-down ends (belt B visibly queues),
packets leave through the gate one per quantum.

## Deviations

- **Phase word:** a packet falling into a pocket counts as "filling" (M4 blinked to "idle" when
  the throat emptied before it landed; with belt B's slower feed this happened on the first
  packet).
- **Receivers skip the step they take an item in** (the sender already moved it that step); without
  it a hand-off covered two steps' motion (a 1-unit hitch at the spout hand-on).
- **The loop's `accept` never refuses:** with no free cleat in reach (a sender that didn't ask)
  it takes the nearest free cleat upstream and counts it (`stats.late`, 0 in every test); v2
  dropped the parcel with a warning. Throws only when the belt is full.
- **Lock target:** the centrifuge syncs to the cleat phase at the loop's in point a fall ahead
  (`phaseAt(uIn, fall)`), not to `quanta()` at the read point as v2 A did; the fall then barely
  bends (≤ 0.56 units).
- **Belt band:** one closed band mesh (runs and wraps) with slats as a UV line set, instead of v2's
  run slabs, half-drum prisms and half cores; drum cores are full cylinders. Flanges don't turn
  (v2 doesn't either; the plan's "drums spin" is not in v2).
- **Hanging parcels keep the drum's 180° turn** (pitch π) instead of v2's switch to an upright box
  on the bottom run: same geometry, no seam jump; the strap mark is drawn on both broad faces. At
  the drop the parcel becomes upright again: its tape band moves 0.7 (v2 has the same jump).
- **Rates:** the line's source is v2's centrifuge-page feed (8 packets per 11.7 s), not the v2
  factory's packer rate (≈ 0.27/s); both are in `RATES` for M11. HOLD 6, loop 32, top belts 14.
- **Lamps:** the conveyor lamps are v2's lit lenses without a coloured glow (the plan's table says
  Lathe + Glow; v2's part draws only the lit lens).
- Packets enter from a hopper (v2's conveyor page has one) and leave into a closed crate (v2: an
  open bin where they fade; v4 pools have no per-instance opacity). Both are stand-ins.

## Problems and workarounds

- **The band's `width` (belt width) clashed with the blocks' `width` (line width) prop**, so its
  edge lines were 18 px wide and painted the whole belt grey. Renamed the band's field to `across`.
- v2's built `conveyor.html` has an older runtime without `FACTORY.onFrame`, so its reference frames
  are at wall-clock time (q 74 vs v4's q 79 seek), compared by state, not by the millisecond.
- iOS screenshots and the recording come in the device's portrait framebuffer; screenshots use
  argent's `rotation`, the recording was transposed with ffmpeg (2622 × 1206, the phone's own
  ≈ 19.5:9 landscape, not exactly 16:9).
- Hermes `debugger-evaluate` doesn't await promises; perf windows were read in a second call.

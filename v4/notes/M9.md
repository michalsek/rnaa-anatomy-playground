# M9 input path (bead source → belt A → packer → belt B), with the M8 review fixes: result

*2026-10-14. Follows [M5-M11-plan.md](M5-M11-plan.md) M9 (census §1.3, layout §2.1) and the M8 code-quality
review. Verified by eye on web (the built-in browser pane, light and dark, v2's `parts/packer/packer-light.html`
served read-only next to it, `assembly/shots/z-A-packer*`) and on the iOS simulator (iPhone 18 Pro, landscape).
Screenshots, the contact sheet and the recording live in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.*

## Verdict

**Go.** The line now starts at a bead source (the phone dispenser's stand-in) over belt A. Beads ride belt A,
roll over a transfer plate into the packer's gutter and settle in its three pockets, lighting the React-blue LEDs.
The picker lowers its vacuum bar, lifts all three, traverses at the gate height and drops them into the open carton
at S1. The walking beam indexes the cartons: S0 is erected from a blank the pusher slides out of the magazine (its
flaps spring open once the pad clears them), S1 is filled, and at S2 the folder closes the minor and major flaps and
runs the tape head across. Taped, the carton is a packet; the next stroke pushes it off the lane nose onto belt B,
which feeds the centrifuge as before. Kinds go control → graph → data, as the magazine's stack shows.

Go/no-go:
- **Beads enter the gutter:** by eye (`web-packer-*`, the iOS recording) and in the tests: every hand-off belt A →
  packer and packer → belt B moves less than 0.075 per 1/120 s step; beads in the feed, gutter and on the cups never
  overlap (closest 4.73 for 4.8 beads).
- **The arms pick, place and fold with no pops or clashes:** the arm test runs both arms through six packets at
  240 Hz frames: no target out of reach, no joint turns more than 0.04 rad per frame, the arms' centrelines stay
  ≥ 10.2 apart, carried beads clear the gutter's walls by ≥ 2.4. One fix was needed (the folder's home, below), so
  the stop rule's keyframed fallback was not used.
- **The carton closes and becomes a packet on belt B:** by eye (`web-lane-*`, `web-folder-17.05-tape`,
  `web-line-packer-B-30.35`) and tested (3 beads per packet, round-robin kinds, conservation).
- **The centrifuge downstream still runs its §A cycle:** the §A tests now also run on the line, fed by the packer,
  for both controllers (below).

Checkpoints: A `1014f33` (sim, IK helper, bead source, graphs, review fixes, tests), B/C `d52f6f1` (machine,
belt A, scenes, views, app review fixes), wrap-up in the last commit.

## Deviations

- **Transfer plate.** v2 flung beads 18.8 units from belt A's head to the gutter in 0.34 s (≈ 55 u/s from the
  belt's 14). v4 adds a sloped plate with low guards from over the head drum onto the gutter's lip, on a cross bar
  over belt A's rails; beads roll over it at up to 30 u/s and spring into their slots. At v2's 12 u/s the feed was
  the bottleneck (17 packets in v2's 60 s check instead of 20).
- **The folder's home** is [12, 17, 228] local, over the lane's +x edge at S2 (v2: [23, 14, 229], over the
  turret's own axis, so the approach and return slewed the turret 138° in 0.3 s, up to 29 rad/s; now ≈ 20°).
  Tucked upright over [16, 17] it stood across S1 on screen, so it isn't.
- **The gutter holds 5** (3 pockets + 2 waiting at bead pitch); belt A holds when it is full. v2 queued beads
  overlapping at 1.2 spacing without limit. The tempo is v2's (0.25 per waiting bead, +0.8 at most) on a
  progress clock; the queued beads keep their slots for the first 0.14 s of the lift, so they don't roll under
  the beads still rising off the pockets.
- **Cups at the pocket pitch** (4.8, v2 5): no 0.2 jump when the beads leave the pockets. The cups' 12% squeeze is
  not animated.
- **The carton becomes a packet at taping** (shape r 0 → r 0.6, the band seam appears; v2 crossfaded over 0.35 s
  under the band fade-in); the packet item is made then and the beads are consumed then.
- **The top bead** settles to 0.35 (v2 0.5) of its height as the lid closes, so it stays under the flaps.
- **The packet's drop onto belt B** keeps v2's: handed on at rest 8 above belt B at y 27 (local), it lands on belt
  B's in point 7 further on (belt B can't take it under the nose).
- **The bead source** is a hopper over belt A's in point with the dispenser's timing (M10 builds the dispenser).
- **The packer page** stands on a floor at 120 as v2's (`floor` on the stand, belt legs and the hopper); v2's page
  had belt stubs.
- **Cables** are sagging tubes; the drag chains' links are all graphite (no tone alternation).

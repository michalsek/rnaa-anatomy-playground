# M3 static centrifuge: result

*2026-10-14. Follows [M3-plan.md](M3-plan.md). Verified by eye on web (the built-in browser
pane, plus one Chrome at DPR 2 and DPR 1 driven over CDP, closed afterwards) and on the iOS
simulator (iPhone 18 Pro, DPR 3). Screenshots and contact sheets live in the session
scratchpad, not in the repo.*

## Verdict

**Go, pending the user's style review.** At `fit` and in the four crops, light and dark, the
v4 centrifuge reads as the v2 page: same composition, tones, line weights, dots, halo, glow,
void and strobe. The remaining differences are state (where the packets are, how bright the
lamp is at that instant: M4's sim) and a few deliberate approximations listed below.
Nothing from the cut list was cut.

## By-eye comparison with v2

v2 references were captured fresh from `centrifuge(-light).html` in headless Chrome at the
"held 6/10 · in sync" moment, at 4×, and cropped with v2's own projection (KS 2.877) to exactly
the v4 crop regions. The `qa/z-*` crops predate the final geometry and were not used for
geometry.

- **Light:** indistinguishable in style at `fit`; the crops match part for part, including
  dots, seams, spokes, the strobe and the void. Differences are state (packet positions, the
  lamp lit in v4 while v2's was between flashes) and the feeder packets.
- **Dark:** same tone set (histogram of the rotor crop: the same six fill tones); after the
  token pass, line ink at `fit` is within a few percent of v2 (pixels ≥ luma 40: 7.3% vs 7.4%).
  v2's outlines still read a touch brighter where solids overlap, because every SVG solid
  strokes its whole outline.

## Tuning round (one pass)

Delta list from the first shoot, compared side by side:
1. **Crop lines too thin.** The `lines: 2` cap drew crops at ~1.2 css px while v2's crops (a
   magnified page) draw 0.6 × magnification (2.2× rotor, 3.3× drive, 5.7× funnel, 6.4× instr).
   Fix: the crops no longer cap (`lines` stays as an engine option). Lines now match v2.
2. **Tach scale ring.** A forced lathe ring drew a full circle; v2's scale arc is 270°, open at
   the bottom. Fix: a 48-segment Marks arc.
3. State-only deltas (packets, lamp level): left for M4.

Reshot after the fixes: the final sheets.

## Deviations from the plan (v2 wins where they disagree)

- **Frames:** parts on the bowl use `faceFrame` = v2's frame exactly (model x = up), so v2 face
  angles carry over unchanged. The plan's `F(deg, z)` (model x = down, angle + 180°) was not
  used, so the tach ticks use phase 15 (not 3), the crank arm sits at 25° (not 205°), and the
  sync tick and needle at 54° (not 234°).
- **Hand-over point** computed from v2's spout kinematics: (−4.47, 51.14, 100), so the audio
  belt sits at y 51.14 (plan: 51.2).
- **Cooler cradles** x 36.9–46.1 (v2: c ± 4.6; plan: 37–46).
- **Funnel ring at z 171:** a forced knot ring (`marks [2]`), since a seam at an existing knot
  is not inserted.
- **Tach arc:** Marks (270°) instead of `marks [5]` (see tuning).
- **Pads** start 1 unit inside the bowl wall (v2: 0.05) so their contact curves are clean.
- **Motor cable** has v2's connector shells; the oil line has none (v2 `shells: [false, false]`).
- **Slats:** 8 (plan); v2 draws a 9th that wraps to x = −1.
- **Lifting eyes:** closed tube rings (plan) rather than v2's flat ring bands.
- **Gusset:** a polygon in xz with rotation [90, 0, 0], so no mirrored frame was needed.
- **Crops:** no `lines` cap (tuning).

## Known gaps and notes

- On a tall phone the full-height card leaves empty space above and below the figure (v2's
  plate takes the figure's height instead).
- Glow discs are depth-tested: geometry nearer than the lens + 2.2 hides them (fine on the
  machine; the gallery's lens-on-a-box shows it).
- The scene layout is at the 8 storage buffers per stage WebGPU guarantees; the next table needs
  another layout or higher requested limits.
- Warm rebuilds on Hermes are dominated by the index rebase in `pack` (~35 ms of ~48).

## Verdict

**Go.** Belt D now hands its mint packets over the elevator's chute. They slide into the boot, wait for
an empty cradle and ride the front run up. A loaded cradle rounds the head sprocket on the chain, and
its packet is flung over the head into the hopper. After a hidden transit the packet drops out of the
spout onto the speaker's ledge and slides into the throat. The cone pumps and the meter jumps once per
packet. The line's `z-D-elevator` and `z-elevator-speaker` views match v2's crops in composition: belt B
over belt D, the funnel, the boot; and the head, hopper, duct and spout over the speaker's throat with
the mint cone behind. 60 fps on the iOS sim at `fit` and in `elevator-speaker`.

Go/no-go:
- **Cradles round the sprocket without pops:** a cradle is a rigid body in the chain's frame on a
  vertical stadium. It turns about x by the path's own normal, so the runs and the wraps share one pose
  function. Tested at 240 Hz frames (`output.test.ts`): the chain point moves 0.125 per frame (30 u/s) and
  the roll ≤ 0.125/R at every frame, round both sprockets. By eye: `web-output-fling-*`.
- **Packets hide inside the duct by depth:** every pose of the transit (hopper → duct floor → along the
  duct → down the spout) has all eight corners inside the hopper, duct or spout. Above the spout's mouth
  the emerging packet is inside the spout. Below the rim the flight is over the hopper's walls, so its top
  clips the packet. All three are tested; the rest is by eye. Inside the boot (waiting, and the hand-over
  to a cradle) the packet is enclosed by the housing; the only pose jumps are there.
- **The cone pumps:** tested (out first, peak > 1.2 and ≤ 2.4·1.4, the cap 1.35×, rest after the last
  packet) and seen in the iOS recording (`ios-cone-pump-tiles.png`).
- **§A holds for both controllers** (below).

Checkpoints: A `381ea55` (sims, wiring, tests, review fixes), B/C `70d22c0` (machines, scenes, views),
wrap-up in the last commit.

## Deviations

- **The flight is a cubic**, not v2's quadratic through [y + 1, z + 40]. v2's grazed the chain's wrap
  (clearance −0.05) and dipped up to 3.6 below the rim while still over the hopper's near wall. The cubic
  (control points [6, 40] over the shaft, [land y, 30] over the landing) clears the wrap by 1.4, drops
  into the hopper from above and peaks 5 higher (packet top 276, under the case's 280). Its launch speed is
  ≈ 110 u/s against the cradle's 30 (v2: ≈ 75), so the fling reads as a fling.
- **The transit is drawn**, inside the hopper, the duct and the spout (hidden by depth), instead of
  being skipped for 0.5 s. Waiting packets are posed inside the boot (hidden) instead of not drawn.
- **The speaker's random level kick** is a hash of the packet count (deterministic). The packet is heard
  when it is fully inside the throat (0.7 s slide), not after v2's fade through the flap (none needed).
- **The chain strands are bands** round both sprockets (E1 pins on the outer face), so the head wrap is
  the same part. v2 drew the straight strands static with live pins, plus per-frame link segments on the
  wrap.
- **Cradles are five box pools** posed from the chain (v2: per-frame x-prisms on the wrap).
- **Openings** (feed hole, run slots, hopper mouth, throat mouth, ring slots) are void-gradient insets
  (v2: mid-grey fills); a packet passing through one is clipped by the face behind it.
- **`stand`** on the elevator toggles the posts' base plates and the chute's leg. On the speaker it
  toggles the base plate and the four posts with tabs; off gives v2 A's ears and connector. Both default
  on; on the line the speaker stands on 220-tall posts (M10 decides).
- **Speaker views are turned** (yaw −45); the elevator's head hides the speaker from the standard camera.
- Only v2 A's elevator variant is built (`outDir: "-y"`, reach ≥ 12, `access: false`).

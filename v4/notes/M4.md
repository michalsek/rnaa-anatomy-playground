# M4 animated centrifuge: result

*2026-10-14. Follows [M4-plan.md](M4-plan.md) and its §A (the user's spin-up correction) and §A
addendum. Verified by eye on web (the built-in browser pane) and on the iOS simulator (iPhone 18
Pro, DPR 3), light and dark. Screenshots, contact sheets and recordings live in the session
scratchpad, not in the repo.*

## Verdict

**Go.** The centrifuge runs the corrected cycle on web and iOS: fill, intake closed, the whole
tray spins up with every packet riding in its pocket, one packet per belt quantum leaves through
the gate (forward only), spin-down once empty, intake reopens. 60 fps on the iOS sim, only the
readout leaf re-renders. Nothing from the cut list was cut.

## What was built (M4)

- **Engine `sim/centrifuge.ts`:** v2's `centrifugeLive` + stub feeder + stub audio belt, line by
  line where possible, on a fixed 1/120 s step (`advance` clamps a frame to 1/30 s × speed, at
  most 8 steps, drops the rest; `run` is uncapped for seek). Typed state, no randomness, no wall
  clock; `seed` rotates the packet kinds only. `status(out?)`, `outputs(out)` (rotor and motor
  extrapolated by w · acc), `packets(cb)`, `slats(cb)`, `canRelease`, and `CF` (spout start, dir,
  lip, hand-over, belt, feeder, gate lift) as the one source of the spout kinematics.
- **App:** `live.ts` (module singleton: sim, `ctl`, readout, `__v4.sim`); `Centrifuge.tsx` has the
  one `useFrame` (rotor spin, motor pulley ×1.375, gate translate, crank −60° · open, needle,
  both lamp glows, the packet pool (40) and the slat pool (7), readout publish), no React state
  and no allocation per frame. The M3 `useFrame`s in `Packets` and `AudioBelt` are gone. The
  shell ticks every frame with dt 0 while paused, so seeks render.
- **Tests** (`tests/centrifuge.test.ts`, 9): determinism (jittered dt, seek twice, seeds), phase
  order and graph, conservation every step for 90 s, backpressure (throat ≤ 2, feeder hands over
  only into an open intake, `canRelease = false` for 6 s keeps everything, restoring drains),
  §A 5–7, advance clamping, slats.

Checkpoints: A `6840363` (sim + tests), B `6dda215` (web), C (readout, no code change), D (phase
shots), E (edge cases, recordings, perf) and the wrap-up in the final commit.

A steady cycle takes 17.2 s: filling 6.8, spinning up 4.2, in sync 4.6, spinning down 1.6. The
feed (8 packets per 11.7 s) outruns the ring (6 per cycle), so after the first cycle the feeder
bed stays full and the feed timer holds (`FEED_MAX`).

v2 (fresh frames from `centrifuge(-light).html`, factory clock): filling until ~9.5 s, spinning up,
in sync from ~13.5 s, then a long in-sync phase (v2 kept dropping packets into passing pockets)
and spinning down at ~25 s. The v4 fill and spin-up match v2 to a few tenths of a second; the
drain is shorter because the intake is closed (§A).

## §A by eye

Checked in the recordings and in seek strips on web (`rotor` crop, 0.04 s steps through a
release):
- the whole tray (vanes, hub, strobe sectors, shaft pulley) turns, every held packet turns with
  it in its pocket, in the tray's normal direction;
- from spin-up no packet enters a pocket; the throat holds its two and the feeder backs up to
  six (visible on the bed) until spin-down ends;
- the tray drains to 0, one packet per quantum: out through the gate (gate lifts, crank kicks,
  lamp flashes over the steady glow), down the spout, onto the belt between slats;
- no packet moves backwards; the tray never turns back (both asserted in the tests).

## Tuning round (one pass)

Delta list from the first shoot, against fresh v2 frames (`centrifuge(-light).html`, factory clock
2–36 s, and the phase logs):
1. **Belt packets lay across the belt** (squared to 90° from the gate's 95°); v2's lie along it.
   Fix: turn to the nearer of 0° and 180°.
2. **Released packets landed on a slat**, not between two (measured in the sim: centre within
   0.6 of a slat). Cause: v2 starts its unwrapped belt phase `S` at 0 rather than at the phase,
   so the lock aims at the initial belt phase (0.29 here). Fix: `S` starts at the phase; packets
   now land 8.4 behind the slat ahead (of 12). The timeline moved by ~0.3 s from in-sync on.
3. The throat shows two stacked packets above the funnel during the spin, where v2 at the same
   moment shows at most one: state, not look (§A closes the intake with the throat full).
4. Composition, tones, lamp, needle, gate and crank cues match the v2 frames; no change.

Reshot after the fixes: both recordings, the phase shots from 13.5 s on, the sheets.

## Deviations

**From v2 (the user's §A correction and its addendum):**
- Intake closed from spin-up to spin-down: `canAccept = mode === 'index' && queue < QMAX`; v2's
  in-sync drops into passing pockets (L1130–1143) are not ported; `put` throws outside `index`.
- `emptyFor` does not require an empty throat (the throat holds up to 2 while the intake is closed).
- Release angle: v2's `a0 + (GATE − a0)·ease(age/TA)` used the unwrapped `a0` (the rotor angle
  keeps growing), so `GATE − a0` was many turns and the released packet swept backwards through
  several turns in 0.28 s: v2's "one packet spinning the wrong way".
- **Release ride, vs the addendum's formula:** `a = ride + (G* − ride)·ease(τ/TA)` with a live
  `ride` overshoots the gate by ~1.0° and swings back (the pocket passes the gate at TA/2, the
  release starts w·TA/2 before it). v4 uses `ride = min(pocket(A), G*)` and eases over TA/2:
  same start (on the live pocket, with its speed), reaches G* when the pocket does, with zero
  speed, never backwards. The radial path (23 → 31.4 from 0.08 s) is v2's.
- `A` never decreases (guarded every step; index targets are always ahead, as in v2).
- Landing guard: if the target pocket and the one at the spout are both taken, the first free
  pocket (a safety net; `put` into a taken pocket throws, and the 90 s test runs never threw).
- `FEED_MAX = 6`: the feed timer holds while the bed is full; v2's feeder bed grows without bound.

**Other, from the plan:**
- The belt phase `S` starts at the phase, not 0 (tuning 2): v2's lock landed packets on a slat.
- Slats: 7 at `x = 9 − mod(along + 12j, 84)` (addendum); v2 draws them mod 86, which drifts
  against its own `quanta()`.
- Seeds rotate the kinds only (the 8 + 5 feed pattern stays), so the phase log is identical for
  every seed (plan test 1); v2 has no seed.
- The arc into the funnel starts at the bed top (z 191.4), not v2's z 192 (a 0.6 pop at the
  hand-over); its yaw eases 0 → −90° (v2 snaps).
- Feeder packets lie with yaw 0 (v2 `packetSvg`); M3 had 90°.
- Belt packets: yaw 95° in the fall, then turned at 8/s to lie along the belt (the nearer of 0
  and 180°; v2 snaps to 0). The plan said the nearest 90°, which left them across the belt (tuning).
- Render extrapolation: everything (rotor, motor, time-based poses, falling and belt packets)
  is drawn at `t + acc`, so the 120 Hz step reads smooth at any display rate.
- HOLD stays at v2's 6 (one constant in `sim/centrifuge.ts`; set it to 10 to fill the tray).

## Problems and workarounds

- The hidden browser pane only renders when captured; phase shots there use `__v4.sim.seek` and a
  throwaway screenshot before the real one. On iOS the first screenshot after a theme change plus
  a seek can show the previous frame; it was retaken.
- argent's live render counter (`react-profiler-renders`) reports tree size, not re-renders, and the
  profiler session's commit file came back empty, so the readout check used a small commit hook
  (props/state identity per fiber between commits) through `debugger-evaluate`.

## Known gaps

- The throat's second packet sits whole above the funnel mouth (z 188–195), as in v2.
- Packets appear at the far end of the feeder bed and vanish below the belt's end roller (v2 too;
  pools have no per-instance opacity).
- The phase word is announced on iOS at every phase change while VoiceOver runs (about 4 per
  17 s cycle).
